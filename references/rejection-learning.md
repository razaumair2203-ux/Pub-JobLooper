# Rejection-risk review and learning

## Before application

The risk review is a screening-objection audit, not a rejection predictor. It
lists non-direct JD requirements, explicit eligibility/export constraints,
visible counterevidence and what remains unknowable. Employer research is
optional and bounded to official sources when it can change a material
decision.

Change the CV only when verified evidence corrects an error, restores protected
inventory or improves a visible requirement classification. Otherwise record
`LEAVE_AS_IS`. Competition, internal candidates and hidden screening weights
are not grounds for decorative edits.

## After an employer response

First run `jl response`. Continue only when reference or unique company/role
evidence correlates one captured JD, verified package and exact submission
receipt. Ambiguity stops the workflow; never borrow another BAE/job version.

Reason through inferred causes in at least three substantive passes:

1. `OBSERVATION`: employer wording, timing, exact JD gaps and exact submitted
   content. Keep employer-stated facts separate from explanations.
2. `COMPETING_EXPLANATIONS`: record at least two plausible hypotheses covering
   company/job, profile and process factors.
3. `CHALLENGE`: add counterevidence, user context, unresolved unknowns and
   revise confidence.
4. `DISPOSITION` when new evidence warrants it: dismiss, retain plausible, or
   confirm an employer-stated cause.

Duplicate/no-op revisions are refused. A hypothesis cannot be retained or
confirmed before three passes, without a competing hypothesis, counterevidence
and unknowns. Without an explicit employer-stated reason, use
`RETAINED_PLAUSIBLE`, never `CONFIRMED`.

Retained lessons surface on similar future applications as review questions.
They never mutate career truth, fabricate a CV fact or claim a probability of
rejection.
